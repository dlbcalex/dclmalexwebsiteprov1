Pastor Dr. Michael A. Dada

◆ Senior Pastor & Regional Overseer ◆

Leading Deeper Life Bible Church Across the Eastern United States

Pastor Michael A. Dada is a dynamic Holy Ghost-filled preacher whose ministry has witnessed the conversion of souls and miracles, signs, and wonders. He currently oversees over fifty church locations in the United States, about thirty-five of which were planted under his leadership within fifteen years.

In addition to serving as Regional Overseer in Deeper Life Bible Church, Pastor Dada serves as the Chairman, Board of Directors of the African Ministers Fellowship (A.M.F.) in the USA and Europe. He is also serving as the National Vice Chairman and Regional Mobilization Coordinator for the Africa Strategic Leadership Prayer Network (A.S.L.P.N.) in the USA.

Beyond his leadership across the Eastern United States, Pastor Dada carries an active missionary burden for the Philippines, where he serves as a lead missionary within Deeper Life Bible Church's growing work in the nation. Through this cross-continental ministry, he partners with local pastors and leaders to plant churches, disciple new believers, and extend the reach of the Gospel across the Philippines and Southeast Asia.

His leadership reflects a broader vision of unity among ministers — building coalitions across the African diaspora, prayer networks, and mission fields alike — all centered on the same call to holiness and evangelism that has shaped Deeper Life Bible Church since its founding in 1973. Congregations like ours in Alexandria, VA are part of this same global movement under his regional oversight.
