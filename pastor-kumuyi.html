Pastor Dr. William F. Kumuyi

◆ General Superintendent ◆

Founder & General Superintendent

Pastor Dr. William F. Kumuyi is the Founder and General Superintendent of Deeper Life Bible Church, overseeing the global Deeper Life Christian Ministry. In 1973, while serving as a mathematics lecturer at the University of Lagos, he began a Bible study with 15 students that grew into one of the world's largest Christian movements.

He is known for his deep biblical teaching, unwavering commitment to holiness, and lifelong passion for global evangelization — leading Deeper Life congregations across the world, including our church family here in Alexandria, VA.
