Global Crusade with Kumuyi (GCK)

◆ What is GCK? ◆

A worldwide crusade, carried into every home by livestream

Global Crusade with Kumuyi (GCK) is a monthly evangelistic crusade convened by Pastor Dr. William F. Kumuyi, Founder and General Superintendent of Deeper Life Bible Church. Born from a mandate to proclaim the Gospel to every creature and bring hope to a world facing mounting, seemingly unresolvable crises, GCK carries decades of Pastor Kumuyi's ministry to a global, livestreamed audience.

180+ Nations Reached through GCK broadcasts

◆ How It Works ◆

A month-long crusade, every month

Each GCK crusade runs from Thursday through Tuesday, with evening sessions of preaching, worship, and altar calls broadcast live and translated into multiple languages. Alongside the main crusade, GCK hosts concurrent Ministers & Professionals Conferences, youth programming, medical outreach, and follow-up discipleship for new converts — reaching congregations like ours in Alexandria, VA as part of one global movement.
