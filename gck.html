◆ What is GCK? ◆

A worldwide crusade, carried into every home by livestream

Global Crusade with Kumuyi (GCK) is a monthly evangelistic crusade convened by Pastor Dr. William F. Kumuyi, Founder and General Superintendent of Deeper Life Bible Church. Born from a mandate to proclaim the Gospel to every creature and bring hope to a world facing mounting, seemingly unresolvable crises, GCK carries decades of Pastor Kumuyi's ministry to a global, livestreamed audience.

180+ Nations reached through GCK broadcasts
600,000+ Salvations recorded
5.9 Million+ Crusade attendees to date
◆ How It Works ◆

A month-long crusade, every month

Reaching congregations like ours in Alexandria, VA as part of one global movement.

Thursday through Tuesday

Each crusade runs over six days, with evening sessions of preaching, worship, and altar calls.

Live in many languages

Every session is broadcast live and translated into multiple languages for the nations.

Ministers & Professionals

Concurrent Ministers & Professionals Conferences run alongside the main crusade.

Youth, outreach & follow-up

Youth programming, medical outreach, and discipleship for new converts.
